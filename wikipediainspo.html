Taylor Swift

Born: December 13, 1989
Birthplace: West Reading, Pennsylvania
Job: Singer and songwriter
Genres: Country, pop, folk
Instruments: Guitar, piano, banjo
Years active: 2004 to now

Taylor Alison Swift is an American singer and songwriter. She is really famous for writing her own songs, and a lot of them are about love, friends and growing up. She started out as a country singer and then switched to pop and other styles.

She moved to Nashville when she was a teenager to try to become a singer, and her first album came out in 2006. Since then she has made lots of albums and won a bunch of Grammy awards.

Early life

Taylor was born in Pennsylvania and grew up on a Christmas tree farm. She taught herself guitar and started writing songs when she was young. When she was about 14 her family moved near Nashville so she could try to get a record deal.

The Eras Tour

The Eras Tour started in 2023 and ended in 2024. Each show had songs from all the different albums (eras). It was one of the biggest tours ever!
